🏠 Homes

Homes are personal destinations shared across your Sparrow servers. Players can save their current position and return to it, including on another server. Follow the proxy setup; the servers must share Redis and persistent storage.

A home belongs to one player. Other players need the relevant administrative permissions to visit or manage it. Homes cannot be made public; use Warps for shared destinations.

Commands and permissions​

CommandPurposeDefault permission
/set-home [name]Create a home at your position; players only. The default name is home.sparrow.command.set-home
/home [name]Travel to one of your homes; players only.sparrow.command.home
/home <player>.<name>Travel yourself to another player's home.sparrow.command.home + sparrow.command.home.other
/del-home <name> [player]Delete your home, or another player's home with .other.sparrow.command.del-home[.other]
/home-list [page]List your homes, 10 per page.sparrow.command.home-list
/home-list other <player> [page]List another player's homes.sparrow.command.home-list + sparrow.command.home-list.other
/edit-home <name>Open your home's information and management panel.sparrow.command.edit-home
/edit-home <player>.<name>Open another player's home panel.sparrow.command.edit-home + sparrow.command.edit-home.other
/del-all-home [--player <player>] [--server <server>] [--world <world>]Delete homes matching all supplied filters; console only, with at least one filter.sparrow.command.del-all-home

Each command also has a /sparrow <command> entry point. The default aliases are /sethome, /delhome, /homelist, and /edithome.

player is the home owner, who may be online on any server or offline, but must have joined your Sparrow servers before. /home Alex.mine takes you to Alex's home; there is no argument for sending someone else. For deletion, put the owner in a separate argument: /del-home mine Alex.

The console can list, inspect, rename, and delete homes, but must specify the owner. Creating homes, travelling, and moving a home to the executor's position require a player.

Examples
/set-home
/set-home mine
/home mine
/home-list
/home Alex.mine
/home-list other Alex 2
/edit-home Alex.mine
/del-home mine Alex

Default home and names​

Without a name, /set-home creates the home named by default-name, which defaults to home. It never overwrites an existing home: delete that home first, or use the management command to move it if you have permission.

Bare /home chooses a destination as follows:

Your homesResult
A home matches default-nameTravel to that home.
No default home, but exactly one homeTravel to the only home.
No default home, and several homesAsk you to specify a name.
No homesShow how to create one.

/home never opens the list automatically. Use /home-list to browse. Changing default-name does not rename existing homes.

Names are case insensitive and unique to their owner; different players may use the same name. Names are at most 32 characters. By default they allow letters (including Chinese), digits, _, and -. Whitespace, dots, and a leading - are always forbidden, even with a custom name-pattern.

Home limit​

Players can have 3 homes by default, counting their homes across the shared servers. Configure the default with max-homes or grant:

PermissionEffect
sparrow.max-homes.<number>Replace the default limit with the highest granted number.
sparrow.max-homes.unlimitedAllow unlimited homes.

For example, sparrow.max-homes.5 allows five homes. A number below the configured default can also reduce the limit. A limit of 0 prevents new homes; it does not mean unlimited. Lowering the limit does not delete existing homes, and renaming or moving them does not use another slot.

Chat list and management panel​

/home-list shows names, servers, and worlds. Your own list also shows your limit; another player's list displays their limit as unknown. /edit-home <name> shows the owner, position, server, and creation and update times.

Every Home list and panel button fills the chat input with a command. Press Enter yourself to execute it. This includes travel, editing, moving, deletion, page navigation, refresh, and returning to the list. Buttons follow command entry points, switches, and permissions; the console displays commands to copy.

CommandPurposeExtra default permission
/edit-home <name> rename <new_name>Rename a home.sparrow.command.edit-home.rename
/edit-home <name> relocateMove a home to your current position and server; players only.sparrow.command.edit-home.relocate
/edit-home <name> deleteDelete a home immediately.sparrow.command.edit-home.delete

Each operation also needs sparrow.command.edit-home. To edit someone else's home, use <player>.<name> and additionally grant sparrow.command.edit-home.other; that permission does not replace the individual operation permissions. Renaming and moving preserve ownership and creation time. Homes have no description or public setting.

Changing a command's base permission in commands.yml changes its .other and operation permissions to use the new base.

Delete homes by filter​

/del-home and /edit-home ... delete delete a single home immediately, without confirmation. all is an ordinary name in /del-home all.

For bulk deletion, use /del-all-home from the console. It deletes immediately and has no second confirmation. Supply at least one filter:

FilterMatches
--player <player>Homes belonging to that player.
--server <server>Homes on that server-id.
--world <world>Homes in that world name. Without --server, it matches that world name on every server.

Multiple filters must all match. An unknown player aborts the command; the player filter is never ignored. Quote a server or world name that contains spaces.

Console examples
/del-all-home --player Alex
/del-all-home --server survival --world old_world
/del-all-home --player Alex --server survival --world "Old World"

Countdown and cooldown​

Home travel defaults to a 3-second countdown. Moving more than half a block, changing worlds, or taking damage cancels it. Visiting another player's home still uses your own countdown and cooldown.

The destination is chosen when you start the command; moving or deleting the home during the countdown does not change that trip. Cooldowns, countdowns, world restrictions, and sounds are configured in groups in teleport.yml. See Teleport groups.

PlaceholderAPI​

Install PlaceholderAPI on each backend where you want to use these placeholders and enable Home. Sparrow supplies them directly; no separate expansion download is needed. The placeholders are available after startup without an extra /sparrow reload.

PlaceholderValue
%sparrow-homes-count_value%The player's home count, for example 3.
%sparrow-max-homes_value%The player's current limit; -1 means unlimited.
%sparrow-homes-list_value%Names sorted without regard to case, preserving their spelling and joined with , , for example home, mine.

They work only with an online player as the context. Offline players, no player context, or a disabled Home feature leave them unresolved. You cannot append another player's name to query their homes.

After a home changes on any server, the placeholders update automatically. They keep showing the previous values while the update is in progress; you do not need to run /home-list. When Home is re-enabled, placeholders may briefly be unavailable while it loads.

In Sparrow messages, use <papi:sparrow-homes-count_value> or <papi:sparrow-homes-list_value>. Use the %...% forms above in other plugins that support PlaceholderAPI.

Configuration​

Merge this section into features.yml, keeping the other feature settings:

features.yml · home
home:
  enabled: true
  default-name: home
  max-homes: 3
  name-pattern: '[\p{L}\p{N}_][\p{L}\p{N}_-]*'
  suggestion-limit: 100
  teleport-group: default
SettingMeaning
enabledEnable Home commands and its placeholders.
default-nameThe name created by bare /set-home and preferred by bare /home; must satisfy the name rules.
max-homesDefault limit, a nonnegative integer; 0 prevents new homes unless permissions override it.
name-patternRegular expression for allowed names, in addition to the fixed length and character restrictions.
suggestion-limitMaximum Home names shown in Tab completion; at least 1.
teleport-groupWhich teleport group in teleport.yml to use. Leave it empty or use default for the default group.

Apply feature settings with /sparrow reload. Command switches, aliases, and base permissions are in commands.yml and require a restart. Lists use the regular home-list command switch; there is no separate list setting.
