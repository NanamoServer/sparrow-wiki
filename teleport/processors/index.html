🧰 Built-in processors

This page lists every processor that ships with Sparrow and its options, as a reference while editing teleport.yml. See Teleport groups for what groups and stages mean.

At a glance​

processor-typeStages it can be written inWhat it does
cooldownpre-processorRejects the teleport while the cooldown is running, and starts the cooldown after a successful teleport.
warmuppre-processorMakes the player wait in place before leaving.
world-blacklisttarget-processorRejects teleports into the listed worlds.
soundpre-processor, post-processorPlays a sound to the player.

A processor written in a stage it does not support is ignored, with a warning in the console.

Every entry starts with processor-type, followed by that processor's own options. An option left out uses the default shown in the tables below.

cooldown​

Rejects the teleport while the cooldown is running and tells the player how many seconds remain. The cooldown starts after a successful teleport and carries across servers.

- processor-type: cooldown
  id: teleport
  seconds: 30
  bypass-permission: sparrow.bypass.teleport-cooldown
OptionDefaultMeaning
idNone, requiredCooldown ID. Teleports with the same ID share one cooldown. The entry is ignored when it is missing.
seconds0Cooldown in seconds. 0 means no limit.
bypass-permissionsparrow.bypass.teleport-cooldownPlayers with this permission are not held by the cooldown. Leave it empty ('') to let nobody bypass.

warmup​

Makes the player wait in place and shows a countdown before leaving. The teleport continues when the countdown ends.

- processor-type: warmup
  seconds: 3
  bypass-permission: sparrow.bypass.teleport-warmup
  cancel-on-move: true
  cancel-on-damage: true
  display: ACTION_BAR
  boss-bar-color: YELLOW
  boss-bar-overlay: PROGRESS
  warmup-sound: block.note_block.banjo
  cancel-sound: entity.item.break
OptionDefaultMeaning
seconds3Seconds to wait. 0 teleports at once. If the player has sparrow.teleport-warmup.<seconds>, the lowest of those values and this one is used.
bypass-permissionsparrow.bypass.teleport-warmupPlayers with this permission leave at once. Leave it empty ('') to let nobody skip.
cancel-on-movetrueWhether moving more than half a block from where the countdown started, or changing worlds, cancels the teleport.
cancel-on-damagetrueWhether taking damage cancels the teleport.
displayACTION_BARWhere the countdown is shown: ACTION_BAR above the hotbar, TITLE in the center of the screen, BOSS_BAR as a bar at the top, CHAT in chat, NONE hides it.
boss-bar-colorYELLOWColor of the top bar: PINK, BLUE, RED, GREEN, YELLOW, PURPLE, or WHITE. Only used when display is BOSS_BAR.
boss-bar-overlayPROGRESSStyle of the top bar: PROGRESS for a continuous bar, or NOTCHED_6, NOTCHED_10, NOTCHED_12, NOTCHED_20 for that many segments.
warmup-soundblock.note_block.banjoSound played every second of the countdown.
cancel-soundentity.item.breakSound played when movement or damage cancels the countdown.

Starting another countdown for the same player replaces the previous one, and leaving the server cancels it. NONE hides the countdown text only. Sounds still play.

world-blacklist​

Rejects the teleport when the landing spot is in a listed world. The player stays where they are and sees a message.

- processor-type: world-blacklist
  worlds:
    - world_nether
    - dungeon
  bypass-permission: sparrow.bypass.teleport-world-blacklist
OptionDefaultMeaning
worlds[]Names of the worlds that players cannot teleport into.
bypass-permissionsparrow.bypass.teleport-world-blacklistPlayers with this permission may enter anyway. Leave it empty ('') to let nobody in.

The server the landing spot is on runs this processor, so the list belongs in that server's teleport.yml.

During a cross-server teleport the player has not joined the destination server yet. Checking bypass-permission at that point requires LuckPerms on the destination server, and the permissions set in LuckPerms apply. Without LuckPerms, a cross-server teleport into a blacklisted world ends with a timeout message after a few seconds. Same-server teleports, or an empty bypass-permission, are not affected.

sound​

Plays a sound to the player being teleported.

- processor-type: sound
  sound: entity.enderman.teleport
OptionDefaultMeaning
soundentity.enderman.teleportThe sound to play.

In post-processor it plays after the player arrives, including after a cross-server teleport. In pre-processor it plays when its turn comes: placed after warmup, that is the moment of leaving, and placed first, that is the moment the command is entered.

Writing a sound​

Every option that takes a sound accepts two forms. With only a name, volume and pitch are both 1.0:

sound: entity.enderman.teleport

To change the volume, pitch, or sound category, write a section:

sound:
  key: entity.enderman.teleport
  volume: 0.8
  pitch: 1.2
  source: MASTER
KeyDefaultMeaning
keyRequiredSound name, such as entity.enderman.teleport. A namespace may be included.
volume1.0Volume.
pitch1.0Pitch.
sourceMASTERSound category, matching the sliders in the client's sound settings: MASTER, MUSIC, RECORD, WEATHER, BLOCK, HOSTILE, NEUTRAL, PLAYER, AMBIENT, VOICE, UI.

To turn a sound off, leave it empty (''). When Sparrow first creates teleport.yml, it writes the default sounds in the section form. Both forms behave the same.
